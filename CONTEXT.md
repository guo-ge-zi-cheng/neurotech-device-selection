# Current project context

Updated 20 October 2026.

The team is choosing one of six devices for the Medical Device Entrepreneurship report: Precision Neuroscience Layer 7 Cortical Interface, NeuroPace RNS, Ceribell Rapid Response EEG, Magnus Medical SAINT, Insightec Exablate Neuro and ONWARD Medical ARC-EX. Keep these six companies/devices in the repository.

The agreed structure is a short README comparing access to information for the five required sections, plus six separate reports. Each report explains what the device does and how far it has progressed, then identifies sources, their connection to the device, how to use them and what is missing. Follow Project spec as summarized in docs/course-requirements.md.

Contacts must be justified by direct use, exact equipment, invention or founding/development. Exablate has a documented London clinical team at UCLH/NHNN. ARC-EX has a nearby exact-device provider/study route at Neurokinex Hemel Hempstead and Gatwick (outside London), plus a London funder/programme route at Spinal Research. Exact London product users were not verified for Precision, RNS, Ceribell and SAINT; their reports identify relevant US contacts. No user-count or popularity comparison is supported, and no outreach is authorized.

The original research snapshot dates to 19 October 2026. Targeted source/contact checks and new patent/economic sources date to 20 October; each catalog entry retains its actual date and coverage. ARC-EX was researched and added on 20 October using the same five-section format. Its FDA, pivotal trial, licensing/financing and local-contact sources have individual coverage notes; the other five devices were not re-audited during this addition. No complete patent-claim map, original funding reconstruction or global current-label audit is claimed. No interviews or visits have been arranged.

RNS offers a clear starting point for public research. ARC-EX also offers direct public records and useful near-London contacts; exact IP mapping and payment terms still need work. Exablate has useful clinical/UK-payer evidence and a local team but harder early funding. The README explains practical differences without a numerical score.
