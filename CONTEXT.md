# Current project context

Updated 20 October 2026.

The team is choosing one of five devices for the Medical Device Entrepreneurship report: Precision Neuroscience Layer 7 Cortical Interface, NeuroPace RNS, Ceribell Rapid Response EEG, Magnus Medical SAINT and Insightec Exablate Neuro. Keep exactly these five companies/devices in the repository.

The agreed structure is a short README comparing access to information for the five required sections, plus five separate reports. Each report explains what the device does and how far it has progressed, then identifies sources, their connection to the device, how to use them and what is missing. Follow Project spec as summarized in docs/course-requirements.md.

Contacts must be justified by direct use, exact equipment, invention or founding/development. Exablate has a documented London clinical team at UCLH/NHNN. Exact London product users were not verified for the other four devices; their reports identify relevant US contacts. No user-count or popularity comparison is supported, and no outreach is authorized.

The original research snapshot dates to 19 October 2026. Targeted source/contact checks and new patent/economic sources date to 20 October; each catalog entry retains its actual date and coverage. Narrowing the shortlist is not a new clinical, regulatory or payer audit. No complete patent-claim map, original funding reconstruction or global current-label audit is claimed. No interviews or visits have been arranged.

RNS offers a clear starting point for public research. Exablate has useful clinical/UK-payer evidence and a local team but harder early funding. The README explains practical differences without a numerical score.

Data under data/ generate the README, reports and source index through tools/build_docs.py. Static course and AI-reading notes remain supporting material. Only this repository is being revised; the separate original course/archive and HTML workspace are outside scope.
