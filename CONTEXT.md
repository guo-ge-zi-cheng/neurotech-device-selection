## Research lesson from the two course examples

On 20 October 2026, the user asked us to learn from Optomed_Aurora_MDE_Report.pdf and Quadsense_MDE.pdf, then retain that method here. The examples were audited across the five sections. Read [the research source guide](docs/research-source-guide.md) for the working method and [the example audit](docs/example-source-audit.md) for report pages, reference numbers, visible feedback and limits. data/example-source-audit.json is the structured record; the original PDFs remain outside this repository.

The agreed question is whether we can build a supported explanation of each section, including honest uncertainties. Direct device records remain valuable, but investors, funders, original employers, clinical care-pathway studies, official procedure-payment records, procurement evidence, comparators and real interviews can answer additional questions. An investor's annual report can directly establish a transaction even though it is not the device company's document. Label each use as fact, background, inference, estimate or unknown; explain what it cannot establish. Do not mistake a lack of device-specific disclosure for a lack of useful evidence.

The specification explicitly allows personal communications and a stated bottom-up/top-down estimate of required funding when earliest financing is unavailable. The examples demonstrate broader source use; they do not prove blanket lecturer approval. Quadsense has positive feedback beside fundraising and the stakeholder map, while other comments criticise citation and writing. Targeted external checks also found product-allocation, currency and citation-fit limits. Do not inherit example claims without checking them.

This context addition does not re-investigate or re-grade all six devices. The existing comparison retains its actual evidence dates. A later review should pursue the guide's alternative routes for each outstanding question before changing an access label. Keep access effort (open web, library subscription, archive search or prospective interview) separate from what the source establishes.
