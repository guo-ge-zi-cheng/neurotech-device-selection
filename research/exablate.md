# Insightec — Exablate Neuro: evidence checklist

**Scope:** Original unilateral medication-refractory essential tremor; UK ET service evidence separate from Parkinsonian tremor.

Matched to the supplied guide on 2026-10-21. [Overall comparison](README.md) · [All required elements](../docs/report-evidence-requirements.md) · [Imperial search outcomes](../docs/imperial-gap-searches.md).

**Strength is claim-specific:** Direct = an appropriate original record/study for the stated fact; Limited = important design/scope limits; Partial = only part answered; Model = conditional analysis; Indirect/Context = background; Mismatch = evidence concerns the wrong product/period; Missing = no accepted answer; Inference/Provisional = our analysis. A direct source can still leave fields missing.
