# Repository instructions

Read CONTEXT.md, docs/methodology.md and docs/research-source-guide.md before editing. For research updates also use docs/example-source-audit.md and its structured provenance in data/example-source-audit.json. Write in plain English. Keep the six selected companies/devices: Precision Neuroscience Layer 7, NeuroPace RNS, Ceribell, Magnus Medical SAINT, Insightec Exablate Neuro and ONWARD Medical ARC-EX.

Apply docs/reliability-protocol.md before accepting new evidence. The targeted 20 October investigation is in data/additional-research.json and docs/additional-research-summary.md. Device reports preserve Layer 1 and append Layer 2, with five topic headings and claim-specific source, locator, reliability, report use and limits. Preserve the original device-records, contacts, access matrix and source entries. Add future findings as dated layers; flag corrections rather than silently editing historical text. A deeper read of an existing source is not a new independent confirmation. Temporary payment support must be checked against the latest applicable official rule.

The user wants one simple comparison: how easy it is to find useful information for the five sections in Project spec—unmet medical need, intellectual property, fundraising, regulatory and reimbursement. Explain what each source provides, how directly it concerns the chosen device and how it helps write that section. A source count or a vague readiness grade is not enough.

Keep README concise. Each separate device report needs a fuller explanation of what the device does, its workflow and its stage of development, followed by the five-section evidence guide. Do not add extra screening frameworks or long repeated caveats to the main reading path.

Every named contact needs an evidenced relationship to a specific report question and a clear reason they could help. Distinguish exact-product use, equipment, invention/company history, and relevant care-pathway or purchasing experience. A clinician treating the target population may inform unmet need and workflow without using the device; label that scope explicitly. General EEG, epilepsy, autism or AI expertise alone does not justify a recommendation. Do not count contextual expertise as evidence of device use, invent London usage counts, willingness to meet or access permission. No outreach is authorized.

Use extra sources when the best device record is unavailable: disease/care-pathway studies, inventor/employer searches, investor and grant records, procedure-level payment, procurement evidence, comparators and genuine personal communications. State the claim supported, connection to the device, date/version/market, access route, actual review coverage and limits. Distinguish documented fact, background, inference, estimate and unknown. Missing direct disclosure alone is not a feasibility verdict; do not upgrade a device until alternatives have actually been investigated. Company funding is not a device budget, a price proxy is not an observed price, registration is not approval, and no separate code does not prove no payment. The specification explicitly permits a stated bottom-up/top-down estimate of required early funding.

Keep three levels of authority separate: explicit course requirements, sources/feedback observed in examples, and our methodological recommendations. A lecturer-provided example or a "Good coverage" comment is not blanket endorsement of every source or conclusion. Do not republish the original course/example PDFs or imply that their interviews were conducted by this team.

Preserve source dates, actual review coverage, product versions, jurisdiction, study design and denominators. Historical FDA authorization is not a complete current-label audit; company fundraising is not device R&D cost; an economic model is not a payer decision. Keep missing evidence visible in simple language.

Update the structured JSON, regenerate with tools/build_docs.py and validate with tools/validate.py. Course originals remain outside this repository. These are AI-generated preparation notes, not an assessed submission or a false compliance declaration.
