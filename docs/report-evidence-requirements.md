# Required evidence: the common checklist

Derived from the team's supplied Medical_Device_Report_Guide.pdf, sections 3–7, on 21 October 2026. This is the team's working decomposition; it does not replace Project spec or create a new grading rubric. The PDF remains outside the public repo. Its identity and review scope are recorded in [structured data](../data/evidence-checklists.json).

There are **31 elements per device**: six in each topic, plus the stakeholder map. Every [device checklist](../research/README.md) has the same elements and five fields: requirement, exact source content/contribution, strength, exact gap, and next source/access action. The overall comparison highlights selected evidence; it is not a substitute for these 31 rows.

## Unmet medical need

Guide physical PDF pp5–6.

| ID | Element | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| N1 | Target population | Indication, geography, period and eligible patient/procedure denominator. |
| N2 | Current care | Actual pathway, comparator and decision point where the device enters. |
| N3 | Care shortfall | Measured failure, poor outcome, delay or access gap despite existing care. |
| N4 | Consequences | Patient outcomes, resource use, costs and distribution of the burden. |
| N5 | Addressable benefit | Mechanism plus technical/intermediate and patient-outcome evidence. |
| N6 | Alternatives | Relevant current options, limitations and trade-offs in this setting. |

## Intellectual property

Guide physical PDF pp6–8.

| ID | Element | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| I1 | Invention origin | Original inventors, employers at the time, earliest publication and priority. |
| I2 | Control of rights | Applicants, assignees, transfers and actual licences. |
| I3 | Independent claims | Identified claim versions; required features matched to documented product features. |
| I4 | Place and time | Families, priority, jurisdictions and dated application/grant/legal status. |
| I5 | Other protection | Documented designs, trademarks, software, know-how or data rights and control. |
| I6 | Exposure | Third-party rights, alternative designs and missing licence terms; no assumed legal clearance. |

## Fundraising

Guide physical PDF pp8–10.

| ID | Element | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Grants | Recipient, award ID, dates/period, amount and project purpose; precursor versus company. |
| F2 | Equity | Closing, investors, currency, amount, instrument and disclosed valuation. |
| F3 | Other resources | Founder inputs, debt, incubator/partner support and in-kind contributions. |
| F4 | Use of funds | Stated allocation and next development milestone/risk. |
| F5 | Funded outcomes | Dated prototype, study, market-entry or first-sale result; chronology versus causality. |
| F6 | Missing early costs | Sourced bottom-up categories/duration/rates plus matched top-down comparables and assumptions. |

## Regulatory

Guide physical PDF pp10–12.

| ID | Element | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Product boundary | Configuration, accessories/software, indication, user and setting. |
| R2 | Market route | Country, classification, rule/code, pathway and why it fits. |
| R3 | Actual decision | Primary identifier, decision date/type and precise scope. |
| R4 | Supporting evidence | Device risk/function matched to reported test/standard, edition, result and limit. |
| R5 | Manufacture | Legal/contract manufacturers, critical suppliers, sites and quality/release responsibilities. |
| R6 | Continuing duties | Quality/surveillance obligations and dated recall/incident searches where relevant. |

## Reimbursement

Guide physical PDF pp13–15.

| ID | Element | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Buyer and product | Actual purchase/lease/consumable/subscription model, configuration, dated price. |
| P2 | Care funding | Commissioner, insurer, provider, patient or other actual funding source. |
| P3 | Payment rules | Payer, setting, date, conditions, codes, coverage and payment amounts. |
| P4 | Incremental economics | Device/implementation/staff/maintenance/downstream costs, perspective and horizon. |
| P5 | Clinical inputs | Comparative outcomes supporting the proposed avoided events and savings. |
| P6 | Adoption incentives | Budget holder, who gains/bears cost, time horizon, throughput and capacity. |
| P7 | Stakeholder map | Actual/proposed flows, participant goals and tensions, plus lecture-required format. |

## Interpret the result

Read the exact evidence and its scope before the strength label. A source can directly establish a regulatory decision while leaving current labels unresolved. An economic model can answer an analytical question while leaving actual prices unknown. A wrong-period patent or implant-programme funding record is marked as a mismatch rather than counted as support.

Unknown is not the same as false or undisclosed. A proposed query is not a completed search. Database access does not create missing private facts, and an additional source does not automatically close a gap. [Reliability protocol](reliability-protocol.md).
