## Route codes and the exact output needed

### LIT

**Clinical literature:** Imperial lists MEDLINE/Embase; use Library Search and publisher links for original studies, methods, outcomes and relevant local populations. [Official starting point](https://library-guides.imperial.ac.uk/medicine-bioscience-resources/databases).

**Actual status:** Database routes confirmed. Exact RNS article retrieved; other proposed queries below have not been executed in subscriber databases.

**Retrieve:** Original paper/table with eligible group, comparator, endpoint, denominator, follow-up, conflicts and locator.

**Limit:** An index is not full text; broader literature does not establish the selected product’s effect.

### COST

**Costs and health-service evidence:** HMIC, MEDLINE/Embase and Library Search can locate pathway costing, resource-use and economic papers; public NHS cost/commissioning records remain necessary. [Official starting point](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/admin-services/library/subject-support/business/health-management/).

**Actual status:** Routes listed; RNS full text successfully reviewed. Other cost queries are proposed, not completed.

**Retrieve:** Cost components, price year/currency, perspective/horizon and event-frequency assumptions; a separate actual contract if available.

**Limit:** A model input, charge, purchase price and reimbursement are different quantities. No database can validate a saving without its underlying effect.

### FIN

**Company and financing history:** PitchBook/Capital IQ for transactions; Factiva for dated announcements; Orbis Europe/FAME for covered entities. Follow the issuer, predecessor and original source. [Official starting point](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/admin-services/library/subject-support/business/databases-a-z/).

**Actual status:** PitchBook reached unaccepted terms restricting AI use; no subscriber record used. Factiva catalog says reader access only, no text mining/analytics; no subscriber search performed. Capital IQ not tested. Orbis M&A listing has five years of global coverage, unsuitable by itself for old rounds.

**Retrieve:** Permitted transaction/source citation with recipient, closing, instrument, amount, investors, valuation basis and stated use; keep unknown fields unknown.

**Limit:** Student-led use must follow the licence. Coverage does not guarantee hidden valuations, product budgets or old transactions. No raw licensed export is published.

### PAT

**Patent and rights investigation:** Imperial’s patent guidance/librarian support can improve inventor, predecessor and technical searches. Use public official registers for decisive status/assignment checks. [Official starting point](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/admin-services/library/find-books-articles-and-more/patents/).

**Actual status:** Route recorded in existing Imperial guide; no new specialist subscriber search or legal opinion completed.

**Retrieve:** Correct original family, dated official status/assignment and independent-claim-to-feature table with unresolved elements.

**Limit:** Subscriptions cannot infer private licences, inventor employment, exact coverage or freedom to operate from titles/counts.

### STD

**Standards:** Use Imperial’s standards guide to locate the exact ISO/IEC/BS document and historical edition named in the device record. [Official starting point](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/admin-services/library/find-books-articles-and-more/standards/).

**Actual status:** Access route recorded in existing Imperial guide; no complete licensed standard retrieved for these checklists.

**Retrieve:** Risk → historical requirement/edition → actual device test evidence → limit. Retain source access and redistribution rules.

**Limit:** Reading a standard explains a requirement; it does not prove that a manufacturer complied or reveal its test report.

### GRANT

**Awards and original research support:** NIH RePORTER, NIHR Funding and Awards and UKRI Gateway to Research are public. Imperial full-text access can recover original acknowledgement/award identifiers; ask library staff to help trace them. [Official starting point](https://reporter.nih.gov/).

**Actual status:** Existing award acknowledgements reused; no new award amount accepted in this pass.

**Retrieve:** Authoritative ID, recipient, period, amount, purpose, relevant output and explicit product-allocation limit.

**Limit:** An award to a precursor laboratory is not automatically company cash or an ARC-EX/SAINT budget.

### EST

**Capital estimate inputs:** Company filings and permitted comparator research can help source staff, prototypes, verification, trials and tooling costs; literature gives trial/design demands. [Official starting point](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/admin-services/library/subject-support/business/company-and-financial-information/).

**Actual status:** No justified numerical early-capital estimate completed; per-device categories are identified.

**Retrieve:** Bottom-up range and a matched top-down cross-check with stage, geography/year, duration and assumptions; separate from historical fundraising.

**Limit:** Private company totals cannot be assigned to a device without an allocation rule. Advice or a generic market report cannot replace sourced inputs.

### SERVICE

**Actual service and purchasing evidence:** Imperial librarians can help locate procurement/service records. The existing exact-product clinical/facility contacts can answer scoped operational questions if the group later obtains a conversation. [Official starting point](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/admin-services/library/subject-support/business/).

**Actual status:** No new contact, quotation, interview or visit arranged. Actual London/nearby connections remain in the preserved studies.

**Retrieve:** Dated configuration/price, budget holder, patient flow, staff time, capacity and attribution; state when unavailable.

**Limit:** A subscription rarely supplies confidential local prices, capacity or contracts. General expertise is not exact-device experience.

### OPEN

**Official records first:** Exact regulator, recall/incident, payer and commissioning records are mostly official/public routes. Search model and indication, not only brand. [Official starting point](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/device-advice-comprehensive-regulatory-assistance/medical-device-databases).

**Actual status:** Historical device/policy records reused; no complete new current-label, recall/MAUDE, global authorisation or payer audit performed.

**Retrieve:** Exact identifier, date, market/version, conditions, searched database/terms and retrieval outcome.

**Limit:** Imperial subscriptions do not replace official decisions. A search returning nothing does not prove no incident, trial, approval or payment exists.

### COURSE

**Course map format:** Check the actual reimbursement/stakeholder-map lecture materials in Canvas. The supplied guide explicitly says its map discussion does not supply the lecture format. [Official starting point](https://canvas.imperial.ac.uk/courses/10384/modules).

**Actual status:** Lecture-specific format not retrieved in this pass; existing diagrams remain provisional.

**Retrieve:** Actual format plus case-specific verified money/product/care/authority flows, goals, tension and explicit hypothetical arrows.

**Limit:** An example diagram or our role analysis cannot certify compliance with an unseen lecture instruction.

## Best use of a student's next hour

- For a weak finance row, first recover an attributable old transaction or award. A recent-only deal collection or a whole-company R&D total will not answer original development finance.
- For a model/effect row, retrieve the original full paper and its methods. The RNS access test demonstrates that Imperial can add detail beyond an abstract.
- For an unknown price or capacity row, look for a contract or actual service record. A library subscription cannot substitute for a quotation or a conversation that has not happened.
- For an IP row, use the correct original family and official status/assignment records. A licence to a database does not complete legal/product mapping.
- For the stakeholder-format row, retrieve the actual lecture instruction. Existing diagrams remain provisional.

The broader [Imperial directory](imperial-resources.md) and [general source collection](information-resources.md) remain useful discovery tools. The narrower actions above exist to close a named requirement, rather than increase the source count.
