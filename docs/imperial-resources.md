# Imperial resources for research and commercial investigation

**Public guidance checked: 21 October 2026.** This directory explains the information and support available through Imperial, how an eligible researcher can approach it, and how it helps the five report sections. It supplements the [general resource directory](information-resources.md) and [research source guide](research-source-guide.md).

These are institutional access routes, not a record of any individual's accounts, permissions, appointments or subscriptions. Public guidance establishes what a service offers; eligibility, authentication, licensed content and any required approval still need checking for the intended use. No private account information or individual login-test results are published here.

## Choose resources by the report question

The following is a suggested research workflow, not a new assessment requirement or accepted finding about any device.

| Report section | Useful Imperial routes | Actual evidence to retrieve |
|---|---|---|
| Unmet medical need | MEDLINE/Embase, IEEE Xplore, Scopus/Web of Science and health-management resources. | Original studies of the eligible population, care pathway, outcomes and costs. Keep disease burden separate from device eligibility. |
| Intellectual property | Library patent guidance, technical literature and Enterprise Lab IP advice where eligible. | Patent publications and independent claims, priority/assignment history and actual licensing documents. Advice and search results do not establish product coverage or freedom to operate. |
| Fundraising | PitchBook, Capital IQ, company databases, news archives and Enterprise Lab fundraising advice. | Dated rounds, investors and grants, then original filings or counterparty records. Company financing is not the selected device's development budget. |
| Regulatory and manufacturing | Standards collections, technical literature, subject librarians and relevant expert support. | The standard/edition referenced by the actual device record, regulator decisions, instructions and supplier evidence. Access to a standard does not prove compliance. |
| Reimbursement | Health-management literature, economic studies, company disclosures and market reports for context. | Exact payer/commissioning policies and economic studies for the indication, country, setting and year. A market forecast is not a payment decision. |

### Standards, patents and practical access

[Imperial's standards guide](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/admin-services/library/find-books-articles-and-more/standards/) lists routes to British, ISO, IEC, IEEE, ASTM and other standards. Check the exact document and edition through the listed platform. Standards are supplied for educational use; do not assume permission for commercial reuse or public redistribution.

The [patent guide](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/admin-services/library/find-books-articles-and-more/patents/) combines public search tools with specialist library resources. Many patent records are already open. Institutional access and librarian help improve discovery; they do not determine legal scope or product mapping.

For ordinary library access:

1. Start from the official guide or Library Search rather than a vendor's sales page.
2. Follow the institutional-login or registration instructions in the [off-campus access guide](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/admin-services/library/find-books-articles-and-more/passwords-and-working-off-site/). Some resources use a separate account or library password route.
3. Check the included content, download allowance and current licence. Use [Document Delivery](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/admin-services/library/find-books-articles-and-more/document-delivery/) or ask a subject librarian when a needed item is unavailable.

[Bloomberg Terminal](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/admin-services/library/subject-support/business/bloomberg/) is a special case: Imperial lists designated PCs in the Abdus Salam Library, with additional Business School facilities and eligibility restrictions. Its licence does not provide remote terminal access. It also restricts where exported data may be used.

## Research Computing: a facility, not an evidence database

[RCS access guidance](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/admin-services/ict/self-service/research-support/rcs/get-access/) requires prior registration. Undergraduates and taught postgraduates working on a computational research project should consult their supervisor, who may register them. Postdoctoral, PhD and research postgraduate users are directed to their group leader or supervisor. Standard HPC is described as free at the point of use; other services, capacity and costs need checking. RDS projects require an active research grant or GL code. The guidance limits the stated HPC/storage services to Public or Unrestricted data.

A possible use is a supervised simulation or literature-data analysis. This directory establishes neither an approved account nor permission to process patient data. Computing capacity also does not grant rights to mine a subscribed database.

## Careers and Enterprise Lab: routes to people and opportunities

[JobsLive](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/admin-services/careers/about/jobslive/) provides employer/vacancy search and event/appointment booking. Current students are automatically registered and use Imperial authentication. Graduate access has separate registration and time limits. Use these services for sector understanding and career exploration; an employer listing does not establish exact-device expertise, willingness to be interviewed or access to confidential information. This directory does not assert any booking or contact.

[Enterprise Lab](https://www.imperialenterpriselab.com/) offers idea development, programmes, workspace routes and entrepreneurial support. [Experts-in-Residence](https://www.imperialenterpriselab.com/services/x-i-r/) covers areas including IP, commercialisation and fundraising; access requires an Idea Surgery and approval for relevant advice. [Venture Mentoring](https://www.imperialenterpriselab.com/services/venture-mentoring/) has further stage/entry requirements. Advice could help frame a business question or capital estimate, but it is not a record of historical device funding. Programme eligibility, investment or a mentor introduction must not be assumed.

## Keep the public repository separate from private access

Publish stable official links, generic access instructions and permitted citations. Keep personal email addresses, usernames, student identifiers, passwords, MFA, cookies, session URLs, account screenshots, CVs, bookings and private conversations outside the repository. Remove tracking parameters from shared resource links. Never commit library password lists or raw licensed exports.

Access to read content is separate from permission to redistribute it, automate collection or submit it to an AI service. Check the source licence and [Imperial's text/data-mining guidance](https://library-guides.imperial.ac.uk/TDM/introduction) before such use. Cite the actual document and locator in a permitted evidence note; do not upload the full paid report merely to make a reference accessible.

These entries were added as source-discovery guidance only. They do not add accepted device findings, alter the seven studies' access labels, or establish any individual's active entitlements. Future device claims still follow the [reliability protocol](reliability-protocol.md).
