# Information resources for our device reports

**Compiled 21 October 2026.** A collection of the resources supplied by the team, the course company/IP/reimbursement handout, and sources already used in our seven device studies.

Use this file to find **where to search**. The [research source guide](research-source-guide.md) explains the method; the [source catalog and review notes](sources.md) record what we actually read for device claims. A useful website is a search route, not evidence that every figure or document on it has been checked. Existing study records retain their 19–20 October review dates.

## Start with the question

| Report section | Start here | What to extract |
|---|---|---|
| Unmet medical need | [Clinical and population sources](#clinical-and-population-sources) | Eligible patients, existing care, its limitations/costs, and demonstrated incremental benefit. |
| Intellectual property | [Patent and licensing sources](#patent-and-licensing-sources) | Original invention, inventors/employers, families, independent claims and actual rights/technical connection. |
| Fundraising | [Company and financing sources](#company-and-financing-sources) | Early funding, recipient, date, amount, instrument, milestone and valuation where disclosed. |
| Regulatory | [Regulatory and manufacturing sources](#regulatory-and-manufacturing-sources) | Exact model, indication, market, decision, testing/standards and manufacturing responsibilities. |
| Reimbursement | [Payment and economic sources](#payment-and-economic-sources) | Buyer, payer, coverage conditions, codes, amounts, procurement and full pathway economics. |

Before searching, write down the legal company name, old names/parent, exact device/version, development period, indication and target country. Incorporation, securities listing, device market and payment jurisdiction can lead to different databases. Public records do not provide a universal five-topic disclosure package. This is a research directory, rather than a statement of every company's legal filing obligations.

## Company and financing sources

### Choose the jurisdiction and company situation

| Resource | What to search for | How it helps; limits |
|---|---|---|
| [Company investor-relations pages](https://www.siemens-healthineers.com/investor-relations/presentations-financial-publications) | Annual/interim reports, prospectuses, presentations, earnings transcripts and archived announcements. | Often connects business, IP, clinical development, finance and payment. Check whole-company versus segment versus product scope, and reported facts versus forecasts. |
| [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/) — US reporting issuers | S-1/IPO prospectuses; 10-K, 10-Q and 8-K for relevant domestic issuers; 20-F and 6-K for relevant foreign private issuers. Search exhibits as well as the main filing. | Business history, risks, financing, manufacturing and sometimes actual licence/supplier contracts. Form availability depends on issuer status; the brand may differ from the filer. |
| [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/) — private offerings | Form D notices and amendments, searched by legal issuer and date. | Can establish a reported exempt offering and amount sold. It is not a complete funding history, proof of all-cash proceeds or a device valuation. Not every private transaction has this record. |
| [Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/) — UK | Company number, filing history, annual accounts, confirmation statements, share-allotment filings such as SH01 and charges. | Trace the entity and disclosed capital changes. Filing detail varies; accounts/allotments may leave investment terms, device budgets and valuations unresolved. |
| [FCA reporting guidance](https://www.fca.org.uk/markets/company-annual-financial-reporting-electronic-format) and its National Storage Mechanism links — relevant UK issuers | Annual financial reports and applicable regulated disclosures. | Complements company IR and exchange announcements. The FCA guidance has scope/exemptions; do not apply one reporting rule to every UK company or trading venue. |
| [Unternehmensregister](https://www.unternehmensregister.de/en) — Germany | Exact entity/EUID, financial publications and register documents. | Useful for German legal entities alongside IR. Check legal form, exemptions, parent/subsidiary and any retrieval conditions; no assumption of a Terra-specific budget. |
| [National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System](https://www.gsxt.gov.cn/) — mainland China | Legal entity, registration and available annual disclosures. | A company-identity and disclosure route. Do not assume public access to a complete financial statement; access and field availability need checking. |
| [CNINFO](https://www.cninfo.com.cn/) and the relevant mainland exchange | Prospectuses, annual/interim reports, financing and material announcements. | Search company name/ticker and original documents. Match the listing venue and issuer; an issuer announcement is not an independent clinical evaluation. |
| [HKEXnews](https://www.hkexnews.hk/) — Hong Kong | Prospectuses, financial reports, announcements and disclosures of interests. | Use the issuer and document date. Hong Kong and mainland disclosure systems are separate routes. |

**Siemens starting point:** the Reports & presentations page lists the 2025 Annual Report and FY2026 statements, presentations and call materials. Its listings were checked on 21 October; the linked financial reports were not all read for this directory. Use them for group/segment financial context, then search for an explicit Terra attribution. Group R&D expense does not reveal the scanner's original development budget.

The handout's [Colfax filings example](https://ir.colfaxcorp.com/sec-filings) is retained as a historical teaching link, outside our device shortlist. It returned an access error in this check. Search the relevant current legal issuer in EDGAR if an old IR address fails; do not inherit its identity or records as evidence for our companies.

## Patent and licensing sources

| Resource | Useful search or document | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| [WIPO PATENTSCOPE](https://patentscope.wipo.int/) | PCT publications and available national collections; search inventors, applicants and technical features. | WIPO/PCT publication is not a worldwide granted patent. This check did not retrieve the interactive search results. |
| [Espacenet](https://worldwide.espacenet.com/) | Technical search, priority/family exploration and publication documents. | A family is a search/relationship structure, not proof of implementation or a single worldwide legal status. |
| [European Patent Register](https://www.epo.org/en/searching-for-patents/legal/register) | Application procedure, correspondence, grants/oppositions and links to national post-grant records. | Follow the relevant jurisdiction and date; a legal-status summary is not a complete rights or freedom-to-operate opinion. |
| [USPTO](https://www.uspto.gov/) | US patent search, application history, ownership/assignment and trademark search routes. | Inventor, applicant, assignee and licensee are different roles; check the relevant recorded transaction and dates. |
| [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/) | Convenient full-text discovery and reading of claims, citations and families. | Cross-check important status/ownership points with official records. Application claims may differ from granted claims. |
| University technology-transfer pages, licence contracts and securities-filing exhibits | Original employer, technology transfer, exclusivity, field and financial terms when disclosed. | A university affiliation alone does not establish the rights licensed to the company. |

**Worked routes:** [Ceribell's actual Stanford licence exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1861107/000095017024100574/cbll-ex10_08.htm) ([review](sources.md#ceribell-stanford-licence-2017)) and [Stanford's licensing annual report](https://otl.stanford.edu/sites/g/files/sbiybj16766/files/media/file/stanford-office-of-technology-licensing_fy-2022-annual-report_final.pdf) ([review](sources.md#saint-stanford-otl2022)) provide different kinds of transfer evidence. For claim reading, see [RNS architecture patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US6016449A/en) ([review](sources.md#rns-patent)) and [SAINT patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10595735B2/en) ([review](sources.md#saint-patent)). The [Terra optimisation application](https://patents.google.com/patent/GB2606208A/en) ([review](sources.md#terra-patent-later)) is a useful warning: it uses Terra in experiments but postdates the original scanner. The [ARC-EX report](../devices/arcex/full-report.md#2-intellectual-property) similarly retains a product-to-claim mismatch rather than treating a related patent as proven coverage.

Read the independent claims, compare their required technical elements with the exact product, and trace the original invention/employer and assignment/licence chain. Published patents cannot reveal every trade secret or private licence term. The original course handout and [example audit](example-source-audit.md) remain the context for using these sources, without implying blanket lecturer endorsement.

## Clinical and population sources

| Resource | How it helps write unmet need | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) and the original journal/publisher | Find exact-device trials, existing-care studies, harms and follow-up. | An index/abstract is not full-text review. Record design, denominator, comparator, conflicts and follow-up. |
| [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/) | Sponsor, intervention/version, eligibility, planned endpoints, status and posted results where available. | Registration is not proof of efficacy; records may be incomplete or updated. A failed search does not establish no study exists. |
| [NICE](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance) | Care pathways, evaluations and indication-specific guidance. | Match the guidance type, indication and date; guidance alone is not a local procurement contract. |
| [WHO](https://www.who.int/) and public-health/disease registries | Population and disease burden, geography and existing-care context. | Total disease prevalence is usually not the eligible device market. |
| [NIHR Journals Library](https://www.journalslibrary.nihr.ac.uk/) | UK care-pathway, trial and health-economic reports. | A comparator study is useful context, not evidence for the chosen brand. |
| Relevant professional guidelines, specialist registries and patient organisations | Clinical practice, service constraints, patient priorities and cost/burden estimates. | Inspect the methods and denominator; advocacy or consensus is not automatically comparative device evidence. |

**Already used:** [RNS controlled trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21917777/) ([review](sources.md#rns-trial)) versus [long-term follow-up](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32690786/) ([review](sources.md#rns-long)); [Ceribell DECIDE](https://ceribell.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Vespa-online-CCM-DECIDE-Study-2020-1-4.pdf) ([review](sources.md#ceribell-decide)); [ARC-EX Up-LIFT](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-024-02940-9) ([review](sources.md#arcex-uplift)); [ACNS critical-care EEG consensus](https://www.acns.org/UserFiles/file/Consensus_Statement_on_Continuous_EEG_in.1.pdf) ([review](sources.md#acns-cceeg-2015)); [US SCI facts and figures](https://bpb-us-w2.wpmucdn.com/sites.uab.edu/dist/f/392/files/2026/03/Facts-and-Figures-English-03.25.2026-Accessible.pdf) ([review](sources.md#arcex-nscisc2026)); and [NIHR TMS comparator report](https://njl-admin.nihr.ac.uk/document/download/2047157) ([review](sources.md#saint-nihr-comparator)). Their roles differ: clinical outcome, workflow, broader need and comparator context should not be merged.

For a concrete version/design check, compare [the completed Terra neurovascular study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00062-023-01282-2) ([review](sources.md#terra-aneurysm)) with [the Terra epilepsy study protocol](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10309-023-00618-9) ([review](sources.md#terra-epilepsy-protocol)). A planned comparison does not provide a completed diagnostic yield. The existing catalog records the actual review coverage of these documents.

| [EUDAMED public portal](https://ec.europa.eu/tools/eudamed/) and [Commission implementation overview](https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-eudamed/overview_en) | Relevant actors, device identifiers and available certificate information; check the implementation state first. | On 21 October 2026 the overview identifies four mandatory modules since 28 May, including market surveillance for authorities/Commission; clinical-investigation and vigilance modules remain incomplete. ## Keep a small evidence log

| Claim/question | Document, URL and page/section | Date, version and market | Connection and review scope | Use and limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What precise answer is needed? | Save the actual record, not only its search portal. | Original event date plus date checked. | Exact product, company, population, comparator or general system; full text, selected section or abstract. | Fact, study result, context, inference, estimate or unresolved; explain what cannot follow. |

Useful document searches include `research and development`, `licence`, `manufactur`, `use of proceeds`, `financing`, `reimbursement`, the device's old name and its regulatory identifier. Search the first development period as well as the latest year.

Use **not found in our search** when retrieval was unsuccessful; **not publicly disclosed** only when the reviewed evidence supports that narrower statement; and **not applicable to this stage/market** only with a reason. Follow the [reliability protocol](reliability-protocol.md) before adding a claim. Keep new findings separate from the preserved historical device layers.
