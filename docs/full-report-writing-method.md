# How the full drafts were written

## Reader and purpose

The reader is an Imperial engineering teammate familiar with the entrepreneurship module, but not necessarily with the selected clinical condition. Each draft asks how a defined device moved from an unmet need through invention, capital and regulation into a deliverable and fundable clinical pathway. This is a set of AI-generated reference drafts for comparison and discussion, not an assessed submission or a compliance declaration.

## Structure and evidence handling

Each draft leads with its main case-study conclusion, defines the product and original market endpoint, then develops unmet need, IP, fundraising, regulation/manufacture and reimbursement. Device-specific terms are introduced where necessary; generic explanations of patents and venture capital are omitted because the reader is assumed to know the module.

The [reliability protocol](reliability-protocol.md) governs claims. Direct records, context, model results and our analysis remain distinguishable in the prose. The original two research layers are retained separately. All 19 independent claims in the six selected patent publications were read for drafting; this completes that selected-publication reading, not a complete product/portfolio or legal analysis. The ARC-EX claim wording exposes a significant product-attribution uncertainty. The seventh draft, Terra, adds two independent claims from a later 2021-priority application as an explicitly bounded development illustration; this does not fill the original 2017 scanner-IP gap. Its original FDA submission, exact London equipment page, manufacturing brochure, diagnostic study and funding-recipient records were checked separately.

Deeper checks also read the RNS economic paper's publisher abstract, its 2021 prospectus manufacturing passages, Ceribell's DECIDE study and economic-model passages, selected original FDA sections/standards and current government quality-system context. Review coverage is recorded in data/full-report-evidence.json. Inaccessible publisher pages were not treated as fully reviewed; regulator summaries or actually retrieved text were used within their scope.

References are numbered at first appearance and include source identity, date where known, stable public URL and review date. No personal communication is cited. Plain URLs remain visible in the reference list so exported text retains the retrieval route. Body citations do not depend on embedded hyperlinks.

Each stakeholder diagram is labelled as an analytical draft. Solid arrows show stated roles; conditional payer arrows are explicit. It does not document every contract or replace the unavailable lecture format. No interview, clinical visit or outreach has occurred.

## Limits and quality checks

Missing data were not filled with plausible-sounding figures. In particular, no device budget was inferred from total company capital, no model result was presented as observed savings and no general provider fee was used as a device price. Where earliest finance remains missing, the drafts explain bottom-up/top-down reconstruction and required inputs; they do not pretend this substitutes for a justified numerical estimate. The [comparative review](../reports/comparative-review.md) treats that as a weakness.

Validation checks seven-device/five-topic scope, citation resolution, retrievable references, word budgets, selected claim coverage and preservation of the earlier research records. Counts use whitespace-separated tokens in the entire Markdown file, including references, tables, diagrams and notices. This conservative working count is not guaranteed to match Word or Turnitin; the drafts retain a margin below 4,000. Structural checks are not independent semantic verification.

The comparison is a qualitative self-audit of these drafts against the specification. It reports strengths, incomplete answers and writing difficulties. It predicts neither lecturer marks nor clinical superiority, and source counts do not determine quality.


## Terra extension, 20 October 2026

The same five-topic structure is applied to the original Terra, with later Terra.X/research configurations kept separate. The clinical value discussion uses a defined neurovascular question; knee imaging remains in product scope but its incremental value is not established by brain studies. A prospective epilepsy protocol is labelled as a plan. Corporate product development, customer infrastructure funding and a parent-company IPO are separate flows. The comparison covers seven drafts; the prior six narrative bodies and research records are preserved. JSON-prefix baselines allow the seventh record to be appended while continuing to detect any changes in the original records.
