# Editable evidence and comparison records

- `device-records.json`: mechanisms, evidence, coursework topics, access routes and gaps.
- `screening-matrix.json`: qualitative S/P/G grades, explanations and source pointers.
- `source-catalog.json`: public URLs, evidence type, dates and actual review coverage.
- `contacts.json`: public professional enquiry leads; no contact has been made.
- `checks-and-corrections.json`: checks, actual corrections and unresolved work.

Grades describe research readiness. They are not a clinical ranking or proof of complete due diligence. Source checks date from 19 October 2026; the repository structure dates from 20 October 2026.
