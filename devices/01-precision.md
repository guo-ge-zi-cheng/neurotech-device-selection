## Product scope and mechanism

A flexible surface array samples electrical activity over the cortex without the penetrating shanks used by some intracortical BCIs. It is especially relevant to spatial sampling, signal quality and translating neural activity into useful information. However, a recording array is only one part of an assistive BCI: amplifiers, decoding, implantation and long-term reliability also matter.

## Clinical evidence and its limits

The reviewed FDA record, K242618 (2025), covers Layer 7-T cortical electrodes used with recording, monitoring and stimulation equipment for temporary use of less than 30 days. It does not establish approval of a permanent implant that restores computer control. The company separately describes the broader BCI system as investigational. This boundary changes the need, evidence and reimbursement sections.

1. Which problem and purchaser justified the temporary electrode before the chronic BCI?

3. Which early funds and manufacturing milestones were specific to this component?
