# Optune Gio

**Novocure** · [Back to the comparison](../README.md)

## What does it do?

Optune Gio is a portable treatment for glioblastoma that delivers alternating electric fields through arrays placed on the scalp. These Tumor Treating Fields are intended to interfere with processes involved in tumour-cell division. The treatment is aimed at the tumour; it is not measuring brain activity or trying to change neuronal firing as a neurostimulator would.

The patient wears and manages the system for long periods outside the hospital, with help from clinical and device-support staff. Scalp preparation, array changes, skin effects, carrying the equipment and sustaining daily use are therefore central to understanding the product. A report that only describes the electric-field mechanism would miss much of the real treatment and adoption problem.

The clinical setting also matters. The device history includes recurrent glioblastoma, a later newly diagnosed indication used with temozolomide after initial treatment, and changing product names. Those should be traced rather than combined as if they were a single first launch. Novocure’s devices for other cancers are separate topics, even when they use the same broad treatment principle.

## How far has it developed?

The lineage includes the original recurrent-disease device and a 2015 PMA supplement for newly diagnosed glioblastoma. Exact Optune clinical experience is documented at Imperial. UK private use and trial participation must be distinguished from routine NHS funding.

Sources for this description and stage: [FDA • OPTUNE P100034S013](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf10/P100034S013B.pdf); [Imperial: Dr Matthew Williams](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/matthew.williams).

### What does the clinical evidence actually show?

The product lineage matters: the original NovoTTF-100A recurrent-glioblastoma pathway, the 2015 PMA supplement for newly diagnosed glioblastoma with temozolomide after standard initial treatment, and the later Optune Gio branding should be traced separately. This brief concerns brain-tumour use; do not borrow indications for Novocure devices used in other cancers. FDA evidence and the company’s public prospectus provide an unusually substantial starting set.

**Practical takeaway:** A strong starting point for the overall project: substantial public business/regulatory records and a London clinician with documented experience of the exact product.
