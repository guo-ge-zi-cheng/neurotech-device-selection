## Layer 1: Original research

Original snapshot: 19 October 2026, with earlier targeted additions on 20 October (ARC-EX was added on 20 October). The text and access labels below are preserved. Read Layer 2 for new evidence and dated updates.

### What does it do?

Exablate Neuro uses focused ultrasound to make a small, controlled lesion in a brain target involved in a movement disorder. An array around the head directs ultrasound energy through the skull. At the focus, sufficient energy produces heating and tissue ablation. There is no implanted stimulation generator left behind.

MRI is used to plan the target and monitor the treatment, including temperature changes. The team can assess symptoms and side effects during the procedure while adjusting the delivered energy. In essential-tremor treatment, the aim is to reduce disabling tremor by interrupting activity in a selected circuit. This is permanent lesion treatment, rather than reversible electrical stimulation or the non-ablative ultrasound approaches being researched elsewhere.

The practical case includes more than the ultrasound device. It depends on a suitable MRI facility, skull/patient eligibility, specialist staff, treatment time and follow-up. These requirements affect who can benefit and the cost per treated patient. Essential tremor provides a more manageable topic than combining every indication and later bilateral-treatment expansion into one case.

### How far has it developed?

The original US brain indication was approved in 2016 for selected medication-refractory essential-tremor patients. Exact ExAblate equipment is documented in a London clinical paper, and UCLH publishes a relevant service route. Later indications require their own records.

Sources for this description and stage: [FDA • EXABLATE P150038](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf15/p150038b.pdf); [UCLH: MR-guided focused ultrasound thalamotomy service](https://www.uclh.nhs.uk/patients-and-visitors/patient-information-pages/thalamotomy-tremor-using-transcranial-mri-guided-focused-ultrasound); [Xu, Akram et al.: FAT1-weighted MRgFUS thalamotomy](https://neurologyopen.bmj.com/content/bmjno/7/2/e001104.full.pdf).

#### What does the clinical evidence actually show?

The original US PMA (2016) concerns unilateral thalamotomy for medication-refractory essential tremor in adults meeting its label criteria. Later Parkinson’s and bilateral indications are separate regulatory events. A 2025 UCL/NHNN methods paper explicitly reports ExAblate Neuro equipment in London, providing stronger local evidence than a generic focused-ultrasound service description.

**Practical takeaway:** A clear clinical and UK-payment starting point with a direct London team. Early development funding remains a harder section.

## Layer 2: Additional research (20 October 2026)

The extra search produces a pre-brain-approval financing milestone, a technical patent candidate and an England-specific economic analysis with a published critique/reply. UK procedure guidance also shows why essential tremor and Parkinsonian tremor must be kept separate.

**How to read this layer:** each finding states what the evidence supports, why it is usable, how it helps the report and where it stops. [Reliability protocol](../docs/reliability-protocol.md). “Context” is useful background, not a result for this product. No new interview has taken place.

### 1. Unmet medical need

**Search route:** Read the actual population and treatment choices in a UK pathway/economic study.

#### A narrower decision problem than all tremor

**Context.** The England model examines unilateral treatment for medication-refractory essential tremor, comparing MR-guided focused ultrasound with deep brain stimulation and no procedure. This defines a more useful report question than the total population with tremor.

**Why this evidence is usable:** The authors specify their model population and alternatives. Their assumptions do not establish real-world outcomes or the number eligible in London.

**Use in the report:** Organise unmet need around failed medication and alternative procedures, then distinguish essential tremor from Parkinson’s indications.

**Limit:** No local eligible-patient count or observed before/after savings is established. Clinical efficacy must come from clinical evidence rather than the economic model.

Sources: [Jameel et al.: England MRgFUS cost-effectiveness model](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9733625/).

**Where checked:** Methods: population, comparators and time horizon. **Evidence route:** New source route. Checked 2026-10-20; source dates and review coverage are in the [source index](../docs/sources.md).

**Remaining gap:** Quantify eligibility and existing care burden in the intended market, including contraindications, waiting lists and patient selection.

### 2. Intellectual property

**Search route:** Search the mechanism that enables transcranial ultrasound, then read a candidate independent claim.

#### A patent addressing tissue aberration

**Documented fact.** US8088067B2 names Kobi Vortman and Shuki Vitek, with priority from 23 December 2002. Independent claim 1 combines a transducer array, imaging/processing of intervening tissue and control that adjusts excitation to compensate for refraction and sound-speed differences.

**Why this evidence is usable:** The granted text gives a technical basis for discussing how tissue distorts focusing. Assignment metadata connects it to the Insightec lineage, subject to official-register checks.

**Use in the report:** Explain a candidate enabling invention for focusing through tissue/skull; start a claim-to-system map rather than merely listing patent numbers.

**Limit:** Not proven to be the first or defining Exablate Neuro patent. Full independent claims, exact commercial implementation, inventor employment and current enforceability remain unverified.

Sources: [US8088067B2: tissue aberration correction in ultrasound therapy](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8088067B2/en).

**Where checked:** Independent claim 1; inventors, priority and assignment/name metadata. **Evidence route:** New source route. Checked 2026-10-20; source dates and review coverage are in the [source index](../docs/sources.md).

**Remaining gap:** Check original patent families and official assignments; verify the exact product connection before presenting this candidate as its core protection.

### 3. Fundraising

**Search route:** Compare an investor filing of an agreement with the later closing announcement.

#### A 2012 agreement and closing are not two rounds

**Documented fact.** An October 2012 investor filing announced a conditional investment of about $31.4 million. A December company release reported a completed $30.9 million Series C: $27.6 million from GE and $3.3 million from other investors. These describe agreement and completion stages, not amounts to add together.

**Why this evidence is usable:** Two responsible-party records provide a dated transaction sequence. The October record’s headline gives a $106.4m post-money valuation while its body says $104.6m; that conflict is unresolved.

**Use in the report:** Add a company financing milestone before the 2016 US brain indication. Explain the difference between proposed and completed capital and flag the inconsistent valuation.

**Limit:** Platform/company finance, not a brain-only budget or first-ever funding. The agreement also includes loan conversion, so the full announced value should not be called fresh cash. Neither disputed valuation is adopted.

Sources: [Elbit/Insightec: conditional 2012 investment agreement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1027662/000117891312002831/exhibit_99-1.htm); [Insightec: completed $30.9m Series C](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/insightec-completes-additional-financing-round-of-309-million-182710041.html).

### 4. Regulatory

**Search route:** Separate procedure guidance and commissioning from device authorisation, and separate indications.

#### Parkinsonian tremor has a different NHS evidence position

**Documented fact.** NICE HTG733, published December 2024, says more evidence is needed before this focused-ultrasound procedure is used in the NHS for moderate-to-severe Parkinsonian tremor, and recommends use only in formal approved research.

**Why this evidence is usable:** The actual NICE recommendations were retrieved and read. They address this indication and procedure, rather than every use of the device.

**Use in the report:** Contrast it with the existing essential-tremor commissioning source. Keep three questions separate: device market authorisation, clinical-procedure recommendation and who funds which indication.

**Limit:** NICE guidance is not an MHRA/CE certificate. Do not generalise the essential-tremor route to Parkinson’s disease or claim a complete current UK device-label audit.

Sources: [NICE HTG733: MR-guided focused ultrasound for Parkinsonian tremor](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/htg733/resources/mriguided-focused-ultrasound-thalamotomy-for-treating-moderate-to-severe-tremor-in-parkinsons-pdf-1809600701173189); [NHSE FOCUSED ULTRASOUND 2020](https://www.england.nhs.uk/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/1904-transcranial-magnetic-resonance-guided-focused-ultrasound-thalamotomy-for-treatment-of-medication-refract.pdf).

**Where checked:** NICE recommendations 1.1-1.4, physical PDF p4; existing essential-tremor policy. **Evidence route:** New source route. Checked 2026-10-20; source dates and review coverage are in the [source index](../docs/sources.md).

**Remaining gap:** Verify the exact device model’s current UK/EU authorisation, standards and manufacturing sites separately from procedure guidance.

### 5. Reimbursement

**Search route:** Use a local economic model and read both the published criticism and authors’ response.

#### A useful England model, with visible assumptions

**Study result.** In a five-year England model, body tables report total costs of £19,779 for MRgFUS, £62,348 for DBS and £3,735 for no procedure; the MRgFUS/no-procedure base-case result is £20,851 per quality-adjusted life-year (QALY). These are modelled totals, not purchase prices or observed savings.

**Why this evidence is usable:** A defined perspective and horizon make the result inspectable. The abstract repeats an inconsistent comparator cost; the body tables are used here.

**Use in the report:** Explain the economic case and identify inputs to question: equipment/session costs, utilisation, outcomes and comparator choice. The existing NHS policy answers a different question about commissioning.

**Limit:** Historical model inputs and assumptions are not a current tariff, local quote or guaranteed NHS saving.

Sources: [Jameel et al.: England MRgFUS cost-effectiveness model](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9733625/).

**Where checked:** Body Tables 3 and 5; model methods and sensitivity discussion. **Evidence route:** New source route. Checked 2026-10-20; source dates and review coverage are in the [source index](../docs/sources.md).

#### The critique needs the reply beside it

**Context.** Published correspondence questions throughput, capital-cost and comparator assumptions. The authors reply that their NHS reimbursement-based costing accounts for capital costs and discuss the restricted comparison; they explicitly identify ExAblate 4000.

**Why this evidence is usable:** Both sides were read. A criticism alone is not treated as proof that the model omitted a cost.

**Use in the report:** Use this exchange to show critical appraisal and prepare specific questions for the already-identified London focused-ultrasound team about costing and service capacity.

**Limit:** Publication/affiliation does not establish current willingness to discuss the work. No new interview or cost validation has occurred.

Sources: [Published critique and author reply on MRgFUS cost assumptions](https://academic.oup.com/bjr/article/96/1150/20220995/7498967).

**Where checked:** Letter and authors’ response. **Evidence route:** New source route. Checked 2026-10-20; source dates and review coverage are in the [source index](../docs/sources.md).

**Remaining gap:** Obtain local current cost inputs and the required stakeholder-map format. Separate equipment procurement from patient-pathway funding.
