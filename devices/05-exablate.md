# Exablate Neuro

**Insightec** · [Back to the comparison](../README.md)

## What does it do?

Exablate Neuro uses focused ultrasound to make a small, controlled lesion in a brain target involved in a movement disorder. An array around the head directs ultrasound energy through the skull. At the focus, sufficient energy produces heating and tissue ablation. There is no implanted stimulation generator left behind.

MRI is used to plan the target and monitor the treatment, including temperature changes. The team can assess symptoms and side effects during the procedure while adjusting the delivered energy. In essential-tremor treatment, the aim is to reduce disabling tremor by interrupting activity in a selected circuit. This is permanent lesion treatment, rather than reversible electrical stimulation or the non-ablative ultrasound approaches being researched elsewhere.

The practical case includes more than the ultrasound device. It depends on a suitable MRI facility, skull/patient eligibility, specialist staff, treatment time and follow-up. These requirements affect who can benefit and the cost per treated patient. Essential tremor provides a more manageable topic than combining every indication and later bilateral-treatment expansion into one case.

## How far has it developed?

The original US brain indication was approved in 2016 for selected medication-refractory essential-tremor patients. Exact ExAblate equipment is documented in a London clinical paper, and UCLH publishes a relevant service route. Later indications require their own records.

Sources for this description and stage: [FDA • EXABLATE P150038](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf15/p150038b.pdf); [UCLH: MR-guided focused ultrasound thalamotomy service](https://www.uclh.nhs.uk/patients-and-visitors/patient-information-pages/thalamotomy-tremor-using-transcranial-mri-guided-focused-ultrasound); [Xu, Akram et al.: FAT1-weighted MRgFUS thalamotomy](https://neurologyopen.bmj.com/content/bmjno/7/2/e001104.full.pdf).

### What does the clinical evidence actually show?

The original US PMA (2016) concerns unilateral thalamotomy for medication-refractory essential tremor in adults meeting its label criteria. Later Parkinson’s and bilateral indications are separate regulatory events. A 2025 UCL/NHNN methods paper explicitly reports ExAblate Neuro equipment in London, providing stronger local evidence than a generic focused-ultrasound service description.

**Practical takeaway:** A clear clinical and UK-payment starting point with a direct London team. Early development funding remains a harder section.
